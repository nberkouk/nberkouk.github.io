I am a Scientific Lead in the AI Department at CNIL, the French Data Protection Authority, where I work on AI safety, privacy and the technical auditing of AI systems in the context of European regulation.

My academic background is in Topological Data Analysis (Ph.D., INRIA/École polytechnique; postdoc at EPFL). My publications are on the Research page; my technical projects and articles are on the Projects page.

Current focus

  • PANAME — project manager of a partnership of 30+ researchers and developers across four institutions, building a unified open-source Python library for GDPR compliance and privacy-preserving AI evaluation. About PANAME
  • AI safety & privacy research — research roadmaps on model quantization and differential privacy (with INRIA) and on the sociotechnical dimension of explainable and open-source AI (with Sciences Po); representing CNIL in PEPR Cybersécurité and at INESIA, the French AI Safety Institute.
  • AI Act auditing & technical investigations — design of CNIL's technical auditing framework for AI systems under the EU AI Act, and leading robustness investigations on algorithmic and generative AI systems (vulnerabilities, bias, data leakage).
  • Policy contribution — co-author of national guidelines (CNIL's AI doctrine) and EDPB frameworks on AI robustness, security and model confidentiality. CNIL's practical AI guides
